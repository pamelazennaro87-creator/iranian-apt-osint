# APT33 — Elfin / Peach Sandstorm

**Also known as:** Elfin, Peach Sandstorm, Magnallium, Refined Kitten, HOLMIUM, Gray Sandstorm

**Likely affiliation:** Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) — Medium to High confidence  
**First observed:** ~2013  
**Primary motivation:** Espionage with occasional destructive capability

---

## Overview

APT33 is one of the older and more aggressive Iranian clusters, historically associated with the energy, aviation and defense sectors. It gained notoriety for destructive operations (including links to Shamoon-style wipers in earlier years) and has since evolved toward more stealthy espionage, cloud abuse and password spraying at scale.

Microsoft tracks significant activity under the Peach Sandstorm name.

---

## Targeting Profile

- Energy and petrochemical
- Aerospace and aviation
- Defense contractors
- Government entities
- Strong historical focus on Saudi Arabia, UAE, and US targets

---

## Notable TTPs (High-level)

- Large-scale password spraying
- Spear-phishing (including recruitment-themed lures)
- Exploitation of public-facing applications and known vulnerabilities
- Use of custom and commodity tools
- Cloud and identity-focused techniques in more recent campaigns
- Historical use of destructive malware in some operations
