# Other Iranian-Linked Clusters

This file tracks additional Iranian state-aligned or IRGC/MOIS-linked activity clusters that do not yet have dedicated full profiles.

---

## High-Priority Clusters

### APT39 / Chafer
- **Affiliation:** MOIS (via front company Rana Intelligence Computing) — High confidence (US Treasury / DOJ)
- **Focus:** Telecommunications, travel, hospitality, individual tracking
- **Notes:** Strong historical focus on subscriber and travel data collection.

### Fox Kitten / Pioneer Kitten
- **Affiliation:** IRGC-linked — High confidence (CISA)
- **Focus:** Initial access brokerage, exploitation of edge devices (VPN, firewalls), selling access to ransomware affiliates
- **Notes:** One of the more commercially oriented Iranian clusters.

### Tortoiseshell / UNC1549 / Nimbus Manticore
- **Affiliation:** IRGC — Medium to High confidence
- **Focus:** Supply-chain, IT service providers, defense and aerospace
- **Notes:** Active expansion observed into Europe and Middle East in 2025–2026 reporting.

### CyberAv3ngers
- **Affiliation:** IRGC Cyber-Electronic Command (IRGC-CEC) — High confidence (CISA + Treasury sanctions)
- **Focus:** ICS/OT, internet-exposed PLCs (notably Unitronics), critical infrastructure
- **Notes:** Public claims often exaggerated; technical impact has been real but frequently overstated.

### Prince of Persia (Infy)
- **Affiliation:** Suspected Iranian state-linked
- **Focus:** Dissidents + Western public/private sector targets
- **Notes:** Long-running actor (active since at least 2010s). 2026 research documented shift to US-hosted C2 infrastructure.

### Agrius / Pink Sandstorm
- **Affiliation:** MOIS-linked
- **Focus:** Destructive operations (wipers disguised as ransomware), primarily against Israeli targets

### Void Manticore / Handala / Homeland Justice
- **Affiliation:** MOIS-linked
- **Focus:** Wiper + hack-and-leak operations (notable Albania campaigns and later activity)

### Cotton Sandstorm / Emennet Pasargad
- **Affiliation:** IRGC-EWCD
- **Focus:** Influence operations, disinformation, election-related activity

## Notes on Clustering

Iranian threat actor naming is particularly fragmented across vendors. The same activity is frequently tracked under different names by Microsoft (Sandstorm), Mandiant (APT), CrowdStrike (Kitten), Check Point (Manticore), etc.

This repository prioritizes **technical and targeting continuity** over vendor nomenclature. Where overlaps or divergences are significant, they are documented explicitly.
